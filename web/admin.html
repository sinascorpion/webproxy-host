BEFORE YOU DEPLOY

A small setup checklist.

  1. Connect your address.

    Use the exact HTTPS hostname assigned by your host, or add a DNS-verified custom domain in Domains. You cannot create arbitrary subdomains under a host-owned domain here.

  2. Allow the web carriers.

    Route the assigned public port to this app. Your host must support WebSocket upgrades, streaming HTTPS, long polls, and outbound TCP connections to Telegram.

  3. Keep your data.

    Persist DATA_DIR across updates. It contains profiles, Secrets, the admin route, and the core configuration. Store ADMIN_PASSWORD in Environment.

  4. Optional sponsor channel.

    Generate or enter your Secret first. Register it with @MTProxybot, then paste its 32-character sponsor tag into the matching profile.

The app runs without root, systemd, Certbot, or NGINX. It does not manage your hosting account or purchase certificates.
